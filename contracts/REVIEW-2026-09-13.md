## Confirmed findings

### Critical

**#1 Concurrent pending spins are all priced on the pre-draw pool; a player can buy out the whole drum for k x (1+surcharge) x harmonic mean**  
`jerkz-gacha/contracts/src/GachaMachine.sol:203`

`spin` admits up to `available` pending spins and quotes each at `expectedValue()` of the current pool, but draws are without replacement and cheap prizes leave first, so the j-th pending spin draws from the expensive remainder while paying the same price. GACHA-SPEC §5.3's 'pool a pending player draws from is exactly the pool quoted' only holds for the first pending spin.

*Scenario.* Pool = 1 pack (40 USDC) + 4 Jerks (0.4 USDC). Quote 0.9975 USDC. Player fires 5 spins in one block; every settlement hands over a prize regardless of the words; player paid 4.99 USDC for 41.6 USDC of prizes. Cross-player variant: a searcher spins whenever someone else's spin is pending on a {jackpot, cheap} pool and takes the jackpot at the cheap price with ~99% probability.

*Fix.* Price each spin against the pool it will actually draw from: simplest is `if (pendingSpins != 0) revert QueueBusy()` in `spin`; better is escrow `maxPrice`, reprice at settlement from the pool actually drawn, refund the difference into `refundOf`, and void+refund if the settlement price exceeds the escrow. Update GACHA-SPEC §5.3/§9 and add an invariant that Σ paid over concurrent spins ≥ (1+surcharge) x E[Σ value delivered].

*Verification.* Confirmed by reading `spin`/`fulfillRandomness`: price comes from the live pool and up to `available` spins may be pending at once; each settlement removes a prize regardless of the words.

### High

**#2 A reverting prize transfer inside the gacha settlement callback (PackCollection blacklist) leaves the spin Pending forever and freezes every depositor's withdrawals**  
`jerkz-gacha/contracts/src/GachaMachine.sol:266`

`fulfillRandomness` pushes the prize with a bare `transferFrom`; `PackCollection._update` reverts `TransferBlocked` when the underlying blacklists `from` (the machine) or `to` (the winner). Both real sources catch the revert and mark the request Failed, `retry` re-reverts, `refund` is refused (`isPending` false), `rescue` refuses Available prizes, and `pendingSpins` never returns to 0, so `withdraw` reverts `WouldStrandPendingSpin` and `activateStaged` reverts `QueueBusy` for the life of the contract. The vault already wraps `deliverOne` in try/catch; the gacha does not. Live: the cirBTC pack (0x723e…72C2) is an eligible collection.

*Scenario.* Player spins; before the reveal Circle blacklists the player (or the machine). Reveal lands, callback reverts, source status Failed. From then on retry/refund/rescue all revert, every other depositor's Jerks cannot be withdrawn, new deposits stay Staged, and spins keep selling while nobody can leave.

*Fix.* Do all accounting first, then `try transferFrom(...) {} catch { unclaimedPrize[prizeId] = winner; emit DeliveryDeferred }`; add `claimPrize(prizeId)` / `claimPrizeTo` for the winner and an owner `deliverWon`. Add a bounded escape (timelocked owner `forceRefund`/`voidSpin`) for a spin whose callback can never succeed. Regression test with PackCollection + MockRestrictedERC20 under OperatorCommitReveal.

*Verification.* Confirmed: `reveal` stores a reverting callback as Failed, `retry` hits the same blacklist revert, `refund` requires the request to still be open, so `pendingSpins` never drops and `withdraw`/`activateStaged` stay blocked.

**#3 PythEntropySource catch block is starved after a consumer out-of-gas: Entropy publishes the word in CallbackFailed while the adapter still reports the request pending**  
`contracts/src/randomness/PythEntropySource.sol:133`

`entropyCallback` forwards all gas to `fulfillRandomness`; on a consumer OOG the catch has only 1/64 of the limit (3-23k at the spec's 200k/250k/1.5M) but needs ~27k (two SSTOREs + event), so the whole `_entropyCallback` reverts. Upstream `Entropy.revealWithCallback` then emits `CallbackFailed(..., randomNumber, ...)` and marks CALLBACK_FAILED, while the adapter's status stays Open, `pendingWords` empty, `isPending` true. Every consumer escape hatch keys off `isPending` (vault refund, gacha `refund` by anyone, `cancelApplication` by anyone, payroll `rerequestSeed`), so this re-opens review finding #19 through the VRF path. HybridVault's 20-NFT wrap (~4.4M) is not sized in ENTROPY-SPEC §6, so the default limit trips exactly this.

*Scenario.* Gacha on Entropy with gasLimit 250k-1.5M and revealDeadline 3600. A spin's callback OOGs; Entropy emits CallbackFailed with the number; the player computes the prize: jackpot → call `revealWithCallback` themselves; dud → wait an hour and `refund(spinId)` (isPending true). Same for a 20-Jerk wrap once the owner opens a window with `setRequestRefundDeadline`, for hiring draws via `cancelApplication`, and for `rerequestSeed`.

*Fix.* Bound the forwarded gas so the catch always has room (`{gas: gasleft() - RESERVE}` with RESERVE ≈ 50-60k, revert early if gasleft() < RESERVE) and record the word before the call (write pendingWords/status Failed first, flip on success). Make `isPending` consult `entropy.getRequestV2` and return false on CALLBACK_FAILED. Add HybridVault (MAX_BATCH=20, ~4.5M) to ENTROPY-SPEC §6, refuse `gasLimit == 0`, and add a gas-capped `call{gas: limit}` test against a gas-burning consumer (MockEntropy hides this).

*Verification.* Confirmed by gas-capped scratch tests (finder evidence): the forwarded call consumes the whole callback budget and the catch block is starved at 200k–1.5M; only reachable once PythEntropySource is deployed.

**#4 AdminCouncil is 1-of-N with no delay: any single admin (browser hot wallet or VPS key) can eject the other admin, take every council-owned contract, and instantly repoint randomness sources, writers and settlers**  
`contracts/src/admin/AdminCouncil.sol:37`

`setAdmin` lets any admin remove any other (only adminCount==1 is guarded) and `exec`/`execBatch` forward arbitrary calldata immediately. The council owns vault, nft, work, employment, shop, rarity, gacha, titleValuer, faucet; owner setters that take effect instantly include HybridVault.setSource, EmploymentRegistry.setSource, GachaMachine.setSource, JerkzNFT.setWriter/setVCPayroll, WorkRegistry.setSettler/setShop/setEmployment/setFunding, RarityConfig.activate. One key therefore chooses every future draw (Founders/jackpots at will) and can lock out the legitimate operator with no recovery path. CONFORMANCE §12 defers multisig/timelock, but this construction is strictly worse than a single cold EOA because the lowest-assurance keys sit at parity.

*Scenario.* Browser wallet 0xEfC3…3d14 is phished. tx1 `setAdmin(deployer,false)`; tx2 `execBatch([...], transferOwnership(attacker))` + acceptOwnership; then `exec(vault, setSource(attackerSource))`, wrap 20 NFTs fulfilled with grinded words as Founder/Board, `exec(gacha, setSource(...))`, `withdrawEarnedFees`, `sweepService(attacker)`, `pause()` forever (1.3M JERKZ principal + 26 USDC fees locked). Nothing on-chain slows any step.

*Fix.* Before real funds: owner = Safe (≥2-of-3, hardware keys) behind a TimelockController (24-48h) for all parameter/role/source/ownership changes; separate pause-only guardian. Make setSource/setWriter/setSettler/setShop/setEmployment/setFunding/setVCPayroll two-step with a mandatory delay (announce, apply after ≥24h) and disallow a source change while requests are pending on the old source. Interim: two-step/delayed admin removal, reject council-self targets and `transferOwnership`/`renounceOwnership` selectors without a second admin, do not make the keeper key an admin, alert on `AdminSet`.

*Verification.* Confirmed by design (`AdminCouncil.sol:13` says so): one admin acts alone with no delay. Partly mitigated 2026-09-13 by moving every contract, including Payroll and randomness, under the council; the 1-of-2 shape remains.

**#5 Deployer EOA on the keeper VPS is Payroll owner (withdrawFree, setSource, rerequestSeed), randomness owner+operator, council admin, every treasury, pack-vault owner and 98% of JERKZ supply**  
`contracts/src/payroll/Payroll.sol:89`

Payroll's onlyOwner `publishEpoch`/`rerequestSeed` force the 24/7 keeper key to be the Payroll owner, which also holds `withdrawFree`, `setSource`, `setSeedDeadline`, `setMaxPublishAhead`; the same key is OperatorCommitReveal owner+operator (knows every committed seed; `GrindSeed.s.sol` shows the grind), a council admin, `vault.treasury`, JerkzNFT royalty receiver, gacha service/payroll treasuries, owner of all four pack vaults/collections and the FixedValuer, and holds 982.7M of ~1B JERKZ. It lives as a keystore plus plaintext `KEEPER_PASSWORD` in `.env` on the VPS, and the Telegram bot accepts `/publish` and `/commit` from a username-keyed allow-list by default. Compromise of one host yields the treasury, every cohort outcome, and every wrapped principal via pause.

*Scenario.* Attacker gets shell on the VPS (or leaked .env + keystore, or a reclaimed Telegram username). Calls `Payroll.withdrawFree(attacker, free())`, `setSource(puppet)` then seals and delivers a grinded word so its 10 entries take Jackpot at the 10% cap each (PoC: entry 0 got 95e8, entry 1 NoPay), grinds gacha/wrap/hire draws with the known seeds, removes the browser admin, pauses the pack vaults to freeze cirBTC/EURC principal, and moves the JERKZ supply.

*Fix.* Before real funds: dedicated keeper key holding only the randomness `operator` role and a new Payroll `PUBLISHER`/`KEEPER` role (publishEpoch, sealCohort, processCohort, rerequestSeed, with rerequestSeed also permissionless after seedDeadline); Payroll, both randomness contracts, pack vaults/collections, valuer and token supply to a Safe behind a timelock; timelock or allowlist `setSource`; require numeric `TELEGRAM_ALLOWED_IDS`; make OWNER/TREASURY/GACHA_*/RANDOMNESS_OPERATOR required env in every deploy script instead of `deployer` defaults; set the NFT royalty receiver to the treasury; encrypt the seed file at rest.

*Verification.* Confirmed. Since the audit ran, Payroll and OperatorCommitReveal ownership moved to the council; the deployer key still holds the randomness operator role, is a council admin, is every treasury and holds 98% of JERKZ.

**#6 forfeitStaleEntry ignores Payroll state: owner can strip worked entries from a sealed cohort after the word is public and claw the day's budget back to the free reserve**  
`contracts/src/work/WorkRegistry.sol:312`

`forfeitStaleEntry` only checks `dayEnd + 7 days` and status Enrolled; Payroll._assess treats any non-Settled entry as ineligible (Payroll.sol:214). Because Payroll is the only settler and `rerequestSeed` is owner-only, the owner controls whether a day stays RandomnessPending past STALE_GRACE, then removes members after `epoch(day).word` is visible, changing the denominator for everyone else and returning the committed budget to `free` where `withdrawFree` can take it. Spec §4.3/§6.3/§7.2 forbid any post-acceptance change to eligibility or denominator. It also bites non-maliciously: an 'unblock' forfeit after a >7-day outage voids pay for every entry it touches once the seed lands.

*Scenario.* Day D published at 1,000 CRCL; players work; D seals; owner does not rerequest for 7 days, calls forfeitStaleEntry on every entry, lets the word land, processCohort finalizes: awarded=0, free=1,000, withdrawFree succeeds. Partial variant: alice forfeited, bob's identical entry takes the full 10% cap (PoC logs 'alice award: 0, bob award: 9500000000').

*Fix.* Gate forfeitStaleEntry on the funding module: extend IEpochFunding with `isSettling(day)`/`isSealed(day)` and revert while the day is Sealed/RandomnessPending/Assessing (forfeit only when the epoch is None or Finalized, or require `paused()` like cancelShift). Alternatively have Payroll keep entries forfeited after `sealedAt` eligible with their frozen weight. Independently make rerequestSeed permissionless after seedDeadline so no single key can hold a day past the grace.

*Verification.* Confirmed by reading `forfeitStaleEntry` (no funding-state check) and `_assess` (a non-Settled entry is skipped, its share never leaves the free reserve).

### Medium

**#7 Under Pyth Entropy `isPending` cannot mean 'word unknown': hash-chain reveals make a stuck request's word computable, and the VRF runbook never disables the deadline refund/cancel paths**  
`contracts/src/randomness/PythEntropySource.sol:100`

Entropy provider contributions form one backward hash chain (`constructProviderCommitment` = iterated keccak), so any later revelation yields every earlier one, and `userContribution` (our clientSeed) is public. When Fortuna skips or fails a sequence and reveals a later one, the skipped request's word is public while the adapter's `isPending` (reads only local status) is true. Consumers still honour timeout refunds: gacha `refund` (revealDeadline 3600 on chain), vault `refundUncommittedRequest` (refundDeadline 3600) plus owner `setRequestRefundDeadline`, `cancelApplication` (1 day, anyone), `rerequestSeed`. Spec §2.2 forbids exactly this; ENTROPY-SPEC §9 only calls `setSource` and never zeroes any deadline.

*Scenario.* Spin n's reveal reverts and Fortuna reveals n+1..n+k. The player hashes x_{n+k} k times, computes the prize, and if losing calls `refund(spinId)` after an hour; the eventual callback is Discarded. Losing spins refunded, winners kept: the house edge inverts. Same for wraps after the owner opens a window.

*Fix.* In `isPending` also return false when `entropy.getProviderInfoV2(provider).currentCommitmentSequenceNumber >= r.sequence` or the request is CALLBACK_FAILED. Make the VRF switch atomic with disabling timeouts: runbook/script must set `gacha.setRevealDeadline(0)`, `vault.setRefundDeadline(0)`, an application-deadline disable (add one; cancelApplication has none), and guard `setRequestRefundDeadline` to operator-sourced requests. Add the check to the fork acceptance test.

*Verification.* Confirmed: `isPending` reads only the local status; Entropy's provider hash-chain makes a skipped request's word derivable while consumers still allow refund/cancel.

**#8 Operator commit-reveal: once a consumer's deadline has passed, a pending `reveal` exposes the seed in the mempool and can be front-run by a refund/cancel that aborts only bad draws**  
`contracts/src/randomness/OperatorCommitReveal.sol:134`

`reveal(requestId, seed)` carries the seed in calldata and word = `keccak(seed, clientSeed, requestId)`; `cancel` succeeds for any Open request and a later `reveal` reverts `RequestNotOpen`. All consumers gate refunds only on `isPending`, which is true until the reveal is mined. After any keeper outage longer than a consumer deadline (vault/gacha 1h, employment/payroll 1 day), `sweep` bulk re-reveals and each reveal can be front-run: vault `refundUncommittedRequest` (beneficiary; also any owner-opened window under a VRF), gacha `refund` (permissionless, so a depositor can abort a jackpot too), `cancelApplication`. Spec §2.2 'no outcome-based refund'.

*Scenario.* Keeper down 90 minutes; 30 spins and 5 wraps past deadline. On restart `sweep` sends 35 reveals. A bot decodes each seed, submits `refund(spinId)` / `refundUncommittedRequest` with a higher tip for losing draws (Interns, duds); those reveals revert `RequestNotOpen`; players re-roll for free. Every outage becomes a free re-roll window.

*Fix.* Make cancellation two-phase in the source: `cancel` records `cancelRequestedAt` and takes effect after `CANCEL_GRACE` blocks, during which a valid `reveal` still settles; `isPending` stays true through the grace; consumers complete the refund on a second call (vault: `announceRefund` then `refundUncommittedRequest`). While the operator source is live keep deadlines long relative to the keeper SLA, have the keeper refund overdue requests via the consumer rather than reveal blindly, use a private/priority path for reveals, and keep the VRF deadline at 0 (per-spin owner windows only, mirroring HybridVault fix #20). Document the residual in CONFORMANCE §2.2 / GACHA-SPEC §5.5.

*Verification.* Confirmed: the seed travels in plaintext calldata and `refund`/`refundUncommittedRequest` are permissionless after the deadline, so an overdue reveal can be front-run by a cancel on losing draws.

**#9 Keeper marks a request revealed without checking the receipt status; a reverted reveal publishes the seed, leaves the request Open, and is never retried**  
`contracts/keeper/keeper.ts:233`

`reveal()` awaits `waitForTransactionReceipt` then sets `state.revealed[requestId]=true` and persists without branching on `r.status` (the payroll `send()` helper at :321-327 does check). `writeContract` uses a raw gas estimate with no buffer and the consumer callback cost can change between estimate and inclusion (gacha `_drainStaging`, vault `_deliver` loop), so an on-chain OOG revert is realistic. The request stays Open with the seed public in calldata and `state.revealed` short-circuits both the watcher and `sweep`.

*Scenario.* Gacha reveal estimated at 410k; a deposit lands first; settlement now needs 470k; reveal reverts OOG. Keeper records it as revealed. Spinner computes a losing word and calls `refund` after an hour (C-suite wrap draw: beneficiary calls `reveal` themselves). Payroll: day stays RANDOMNESS_PENDING until `rerequestSeed` after 1 day.

*Fix.* Treat `r.status !== 'success'` as failure: do not set `revealed`, re-read on-chain status, resubmit with a gas bump (e.g. estimate * 130/100) and alert. Better: derive `revealed` from chain (`requests(id).status != Open`) and make `sweep` re-check any locally-flagged request still Open. Contract side, make `reveal` never revert after the seed check (gas-bounded consumer call) so the commitment is always spent.

*Verification.* Confirmed: `keeper.ts` marks a request revealed without checking the receipt status; a reveal that reverts (e.g. consumer OOG in a 20-unit wrap) is never retried.

**#10 Concurrent seed commits (`/commit` vs the top-up interval) overwrite the index-keyed seed file and make up to 2n commitments unrevealable; reveal never verifies the seed against its on-chain commitment**  
`contracts/keeper/keeper.ts:155`

`commitSeeds(n)` pre-reads `commitmentCount()` as `start`, writes `state.seeds[start+i]`, saves, then sends `commit`. `toppingUp` guards only `queueCheck`; the Telegram `/commit` handler (:547-551) and CLI call `commitSeeds` directly and the poller runs concurrently with the interval. Two overlapping calls read the same `start`: the second overwrites the first batch's seeds (fresh randomBytes, unrecoverable) and its own commitments land at `start+n..` with no seeds stored; both fetch the same nonce. `reveal()` takes `state.seeds[idx]` on trust, so the mismatch surfaces only as on-chain `BadSeed`/'No seed' alerts.

*Scenario.* Queue drops under 20 → interval top-up starts; operator sends `/commit 100` a second later. Both compute start=1200, indices 1200-1299 hold hashes of overwritten seeds and 1300-1399 have none. The next 200 wraps, spins, hiring draws and Payroll seals each consume a dead index and stall until their deadlines; Payroll `rerequestSeed` burns another dead index per attempt.

*Fix.* Key the seed store by commitment hash (`seeds[keccak256(seed)] = seed`) and look up `commitments(idx)` at reveal; serialise every commit path through one mutex/promise chain; persist the index range from the `Committed(fromIndex,count)` event after the receipt (and check receipt status) rather than a pre-read; assert `keccak256(seed) == commitments(idx)` before sending a reveal, alerting on mismatch.

*Verification.* Confirmed: `/commit` and the automatic top-up share no lock and both key seeds by a locally read `commitmentCount`.

**#11 No way to retire unused commitments whose seeds are lost; dead entries are consumed in order and each request landing on one stalls until its deadline**  
`contracts/src/randomness/OperatorCommitReveal.sol:120`

`requestRandomness` always takes `nextCommitment++`. If the keeper state file is lost or corrupted (documented in keeper.ts header; see the concurrent-commit finding) the queued hashes are dead but first in line; re-committing appends behind them. No operator/owner function advances `nextCommitment` past unassigned entries and `cancel` is per-request, consumer-only. The ENTROPY-SPEC §10 rollback ('restart the keeper, recommit seeds') has the same gap.

*Scenario.* VPS volume lost with 100 queued commitments. Operator recommits 100 fresh seeds. The next 100 requests across all consumers each draw a dead index: spins refund after revealDeadline, wraps lock principal for 1h then refund, applications hang a day, `Payroll.sealCohort` pins each day to a dead request and `rerequestSeed` (after 1 day) consumes the next dead index; settlement slips by days unless the operator burns indices with dummy wraps at own cost.

*Fix.* Add `function retireUnused() external onlyOperatorOrOwner { nextCommitment = commitments.length; emit Retired(...) }` (safe: unassigned commitments bind no request) and have the keeper call it when `pendingCommitments() > 0` but it holds none of the corresponding seeds.

*Verification.* Confirmed: commitments are consumed strictly in order and there is no way to skip an index whose seed is lost; every consumer stalls behind it.

**#12 One shared native fee pot backs four consumers and the free, permissionless hiring draw can drain it, halting paid wraps, spins and Payroll seals; per-consumer gas limits also overcharge small wraps**  
`contracts/src/randomness/PythEntropySource.sol:80`

`requestRandomness` pays `getFeeV2(provider, gasLimit[consumer])` from `address(this).balance` and reverts `InsufficientFeeBalance` for every consumer once short. HybridVault, EmploymentRegistry, Payroll and GachaMachine all share one source (live: all four → 0xE721…FCE8). `EmploymentRegistry.requestApplicationResult` is permissionless and applications carry no fee (spec §3.2 says randomness costs are separately funded by the applicant); applications for closed postings/retired generations still spend a draw. The same shape burns operator commitments today. Separately the per-consumer limit means a 1-Jerk wrap is quoted the 20-Jerk callback price (~44x above the provider default at 4.4M), `earnedFees` never flow to the adapter, and nothing in keeper.ts watches `LowBalance`. ENTROPY-SPEC §5 defers the payable request.

*Scenario.* House funds the adapter for ~1,000 requests. An attacker with 300 Interns applies with each every NY day and calls `requestApplicationResult` at readyAt for gas only; in ~3 days the balance is empty. Alice's 2-USDC wrap reverts in `_requestWord`, every spin reverts, `sealCohort` reverts inside `_requestSeed`, the keeper's payroll duty alerts every 5 minutes, and the attacker keeps draining as fast as it is refilled.

*Fix.* Make `IRandomnessSource.requestRandomness` payable (or per-consumer prepaid budgets `budget[consumer]` / `reserveFor[consumer]` with a house-funded floor for Payroll) and have vault/gacha/employment forward the quoted fee from the player (`applyForJob`/`requestApplicationResult` payable via `quoteFee`); at minimum restrict `requestApplicationResult` to the controller, skip doomed draws, and rate-limit per wallet. Let consumers pass a per-request gas hint capped by `gasLimit[consumer]`. Add a keeper duty that tops up / alerts below N x quoteFee.

*Verification.* Confirmed: `requestApplicationResult` is free and permissionless and shares the one randomness queue (and, under Entropy, the one fee pot) with mints, spins and payroll.

**#13 Owner-controlled valuer is the gacha's whole economic security: instant, uncapped, single-EOA levers let a stale or inflated value earn ~1.21x per prize at near-zero draw risk and dilute depositor revenue**  
`jerkz-gacha/contracts/src/GachaMachine.sol:326`

Depositor revenue is equal per live prize while expected life ∝ frozen value (P(drawn)=w/W, w=1e36/value), so expected revenue per prize ≈ 0.93 x 1.3 x value regardless of market worth. Fairness rests entirely on `TitleValuer`/`FixedValuer` tables, which are flat per collection, frozen at deposit, un-timelocked and owned by the deployer EOA, and the machine bounds nothing. Consequences: depositors stock the most over-valued title/collection (Founder table 50 USDC vs ~0.4 redemption floor) or front-run value decreases; the owner or a council admin can `setValue(coll, 1e30)`, deposit one token (weight ~1e6 vs 1e18) and take 1/(N+1) of every spin's depositor share while essentially never being drawn, contradicting spec §9 'depositor revenue and prizes are unreachable'. `setSplit(0,10000,0)`, `setSurcharge` up to 65,535 bps, `setGate(0)` and `sweepService(to=any)` are further single-key value-routing powers.

*Scenario.* cirBTC drops 30% overnight; before the table is updated an executive deposits 50 packs at the stale value with ~30% lower odds than fair for their whole life. Or a compromised admin sets a 1e30 value, deposits one pack and collects an equal per-prize share of all depositor revenue forever with ~1e-12 draw probability; depositors cannot exit while any spin is pending.

*Fix.* Before production: put `setValuer`, `setSplit`, `setSurcharge`, `setTreasuries`, `setGate` and the valuer tables behind a timelock long enough for depositors to withdraw; enforce per-collection `[minValue, maxValue]` in `deposit` and revert `weight == 0`; cap `surchargeBps` (e.g. ≤20,000) and floor `depositorsBps`; make `sweepService` pay only `serviceTreasury`; have the pack valuer read principal x price feed (with staleness check) instead of a flat number; consider a per-depositor cap on live prizes (spec §12 Q1). Reword spec §9 to state the actual trust assumption.

*Verification.* Confirmed with a PoC: revenue is equal per live prize while the valuer is an uncapped single-key setter, so an overvalued prize collects revenue while almost never being drawn.

**#14 A valuer value ≥ 1e36 wei yields a weight-0 prize: counted as `available`, never drawable, sells free spins whose settlement reverts SoldOut and locks the machine**  
`jerkz-gacha/contracts/src/GachaMachine.sol:115`

`weight = WEIGHT_NUM / value` floors to 0 and `deposit` accepts it: the prize becomes Available (`available += 1`, ledger weight 1) but adds nothing to `_index.total`. With only such prizes `expectedValue()` and `price()` are 0, `spin` accepts `msg.value = 0`, and `fulfillRandomness` reverts `SoldOut` (commented unreachable). Under both real sources that is a Failed callback: retry always reverts, refund refused, `pendingSpins` stuck, withdrawals blocked as in the delivery-lock finding. *Scenario.* Owner sets a pack value of 2e36 instead of 2e18. Alice deposits one; available=1, totalWeight=0, price=0. Anyone calls `spin{value:0}`; the callback reverts SoldOut forever; every later deposit is staged and no Available prize can be withdrawn.

*Verification.* Confirmed live in a scratch test: a value ≥ 1e36 gives weight 0, price 0, a free spin whose settlement reverts `SoldOut` forever and locks staging.

**#17 applyForJob does not require settled work: a fired character's open paid-notice entry lands its strike and career credit on the next job**

**#18 cancelApplication deadline is measured from readyAt, so a draw requested late can be cancelled by anyone immediately after the request**  
`contracts/src/work/EmploymentRegistry.sol:188`

`cancelApplication` computes `at = a.readyAt + applicationDeadline`; `readyAt` is the warm-up end, not the request time, and `requestApplicationResult` can be called any time after readyAt (the keeper never calls it). Once `block.timestamp >= readyAt + 1 day` any request is cancellable in the next transaction while the source is still pending, so the one-day grace shrinks to zero. `setApplicationDeadline(0)` makes every request instantly cancellable.

*Scenario.* Alice applies Monday 09:00 (readyAt 17:00) and returns Tuesday evening. A bot watching JobApplied calls `requestApplicationResult` then `cancelApplication` back to back: application Cancelled, pendingApplication cleared, Entropy fee spent for nothing, Alice loses the attempt for the day. If Alice requests herself, the griefer cancels next block before the callback lands.

*Fix.* Add `uint64 requestedAt` to Application, set it in `requestApplicationResult`, and use `requestedAt + applicationDeadline` in `cancelApplication`; enforce a floor in `setApplicationDeadline` (≥1h); optionally restrict post-deadline cancel to the controller/owner.

*Verification.* Confirmed: the cancel grace is measured from `readyAt`, not from when the permissionless result request fired.

**#19 seedDeadline has no floor: the Payroll owner can re-roll a cohort seed by cancelling an in-flight VRF reveal it can already see**  
`contracts/src/payroll/Payroll.sol:102`

`setSeedDeadline` accepts 0 and applies immediately; `rerequestSeed` then passes `block.timestamp < sealedAt + seedDeadline` in the sealing block and can repeat without limit. The `isPending` guard only prevents re-requesting after a word is on-chain, not front-running the reveal: with Entropy (or the operator source) the revelation is in the pending tx, so the owner computes the cohort word and every label off-chain, cancels and re-requests until it likes the outcome, at one Entropy fee per re-roll from the adapter's balance.

*Scenario.* Owner sets seedDeadline=0. Day D seals; searcher sees Fortuna's reveal, runs the SlotBook draws locally, sees its Board Member got No Pay, submits `rerequestSeed(D)` first; the adapter finds the request Cancelled and emits Discarded; fresh request; repeat.

*Verification.* Confirmed: `setSeedDeadline` has no floor and `rerequestSeed` only needs `isPending`, so the owner can re-roll a cohort seed.

**#20 Keeper settle loop only looks at the last three days and aborts the whole tick (including publish) on the first revert, leaving days stuck with budget committed**  
`contracts/keeper/keeper.ts:334`

`settle()` iterates `d in [today-3, today)`; any older day still Funded/RandomnessPending/Assessing/Assigning is never revisited, its budget stays in `committed` and shrinks every later `free`-based publish. `send()` throws on a reverted tx, there is no per-day try/catch, and `payrollDuty` runs `settle()` then `publish()` in one try block, so one persistently reverting day (NoCommitments, InsufficientFeeBalance, NoSource, revoked settler role) blocks settlement of later days and skips `publish()` every tick; after maxPublishAhead days nobody can clock in.

*Scenario.* Keeper host down four days: day today-4 is still Funded with 800 CRCL committed forever unless someone runs cast by hand. Or a single day's seal keeps reverting: every 5-minute tick throws at today-3, today+1 is never published, and after two days all Clock-Ins are rejected 'unfunded' while the treasury is full.

*Fix.* Persist an 'oldest non-finalized day' cursor (or scan back until None/Finalized) instead of a fixed window; wrap each day's actions in its own try/catch; run `publish()` regardless of `settle()` errors; alert on any day non-final past a threshold.

*Verification.* Confirmed: the keeper's settle window is a fixed `today-3..today-1` and one reverting day aborts the whole tick including publishing.

**#21 Keeper processes one 50-entry batch per day per 5-minute tick, so a busy day takes hours to finalize and claims stay locked**  
`contracts/keeper/keeper.ts:351`

`settle()` sends exactly one `processCohort(d, 50)` per non-final day per invocation and `payrollDuty` runs every 5 minutes. Assessment costs ~171k gas/entry and assignment ~25k, so a day with N entries needs 2*ceil(N/50) calls: 1,000 entries → ~3.3h, 10,000 → ~33h. Claims are gated on Finalized, so the spec's 'result set, unlocks at [time]' degrades to 'result pending' for most of a day and backlogs compound.

*Scenario.* 5,000 players clock in Monday. Tuesday 09:30 seal, seed in seconds, then 100 assess + 100 assign batches at one per 5 minutes finish ~02:10 Wednesday; Tuesday's day seals Wednesday 09:30 on top of the backlog.

*Fix.* In `settle()` loop `processCohort` until it returns true (or a per-tick time/gas budget is spent), sending the next batch as soon as the previous receipt lands; run assessment and the next seal concurrently; pair with a larger assignment cap (see MAX_PROCESS finding).

*Verification.* Confirmed: one 50-entry batch per day per 5-minute tick; a busy day takes hours to finalize.

**#22 HybridVault refund pushes the native fee and reverts on failure; a contract beneficiary without receive() can never refund and its principal stays locked**  
`contracts/src/core/HybridVault.sol:246`

`refundUncommittedRequest` transfers the ERC-20 principal and then `beneficiary.call{value: q.fee}('')` with `if (!ok) revert NativeTransferFailed` in the same tx. *Fix.* Pull pattern for the native part: return the principal and credit `refundOf[beneficiary] += fee` with `claimRefund(to)` (as GachaMachine does), or credit only when the push fails.

*Verification.* Confirmed: `refundUncommittedRequest` pushes the native fee and reverts on failure, the same shape fixed in the gacha (review #25) but not here.

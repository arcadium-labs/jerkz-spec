# Security review, 2026-09-10

Three independent adversarial passes over every deployed contract (core wrapper; work and employment;
payroll, gacha and randomness). Each finding was reproduced with a proof test before it was accepted.
No fund-theft path was found. Everything below is fixed in this commit and covered by regression
tests in `test/*/…Fixes.t.sol`, `test/gacha/SourceFixes.t.sol` and `test/payroll/PayrollFixes.t.sol`.

| # | Severity | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | high | `Payroll.sealCohort` copied the day's entry list into storage (~22.5k gas per entry); a day with more than ~1,300 entries could never be sealed and its budget stayed committed forever. | Sealing stores only the count; processing reads ids by index through `WorkRegistry.entryIdAt`. Sealing is O(1). |
| 2 | medium | A seed that never arrived left a Payroll day pinned to a dead request. | Owner `rerequestSeed(day)` after `seedDeadline` (default 1 day) cancels the old request and issues a new one. |
| 2b | medium | An unfulfilled hiring draw left `pendingApplication` set for the rest of the generation. | `cancelApplication`: the applicant may withdraw during warm-up; anyone may clear a requested draw after `applicationDeadline`. |
| 3 | medium | Entries the settler never settled kept the character's backlog full forever (no more shifts, no resign). | Owner `forfeitStaleEntry` 7 days after the day end. |
| 4 | medium | `enrollShift` accepted the registry or the NFT contract as beneficiary, and a plain transfer into the registry had no way out. | Beneficiary validated; owner `rescue(tokenId, to)` for tokens with no active shift; `cancelShift` takes a recipient. |
| 5 | medium | `HybridVault` accepted itself as wrap recipient and accepted safe transfers, either of which killed an id and its principal. | `SelfRecipient` and `NoDirectDeposits` reverts. |
| 6 | medium | `available = inventory − reserved` double-counted assigned-but-undelivered NFTs and could panic near sellout once delivery can defer. | Separate `pendingQuantity` counter; `reserved` keeps protecting principal. |
| 7 | medium | Promotion could run while an accepted entry was still open and then judge that entry's strike with the new title's immunity. Partial result batches could promote early. | Strikes are trusted as frozen at settlement; promotion runs only when the result queue is empty and no entry is open. |
| 8 | low | Career results applied in settlement order rather than day order. | Results are inserted in day order. |
| 9 | medium | Gacha depositors could withdraw the prize about to be won between request and reveal. | Withdraw is blocked while any spin is pending. |
| 10 | medium | A blacklisted cirBTC holder could redeem pack NFTs to a clean recipient. | Redeem checks the caller and recipient against the underlying's blacklist. |
| 11 | medium (testnet) | `OperatorCommitReveal` accepted duplicate commitments and a reverted reveal left the seed public with the request still open. | Duplicate hashes rejected; a reveal spends the commitment even if the consumer reverts, and `retry` re-delivers. |
| 12 | medium | `PythEntropySource` keyed requests by sequence only; sequences are per provider. | Keyed by provider and sequence; callback uses the provider argument. |
| 13 | low | `RarityConfig` delay could be zeroed and a new catalog activated in one block. | After the first activation every switch waits at least `MIN_DELAY` (1 day). |
| 14 | low | Lowering the vault's refund deadline retroactively disabled pending refunds. | Deadline frozen into each request. |
| 15 | low | `cancelShift` released the permanent day ledger. | Forfeits keep the ledger. |
| 16 | low | A reverting VC checkpoint hook would block every NFT transfer. | Hook wrapped in try/catch with `CheckpointFailed`. |
| 17 | low | Wraps could run before bootstrap minting finished and draw phantom ids. | Intake requires `bootstrapClosed()`. |
| 18 | low | The fixed beneficiary of a Jerk in shift custody could not apply or resign. | Beneficiary recognised as controller while the token is in custody. |

Not changed, by decision: the `DepositorLedger` round-up leaves at most one wei per spin unclaimable
(safe, bounded); a 20-NFT wrap settlement costs ~4.2M gas and must be sized against the Entropy
callback limit before mainnet; the operator source remains grindable by the operator, which is why
production uses a VRF.

## Second pass (same day)

Three fresh reviewers with a different cut: value flows, token and generation lifecycle, and rule
conformance against the spec plus randomness and gas. 45 proof tests reproduced the findings below;
all are fixed in this commit with regression tests (`test/*/…Fixes2.t.sol`).

| # | Severity | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 19 | medium | A randomness callback that ran out of gas left the word public at the source while the vault still allowed a refund, so a batch wrap could be aborted after seeing its characters. Same shape in gacha refund, application cancel and payroll re-request. | `IRandomnessSource.isPending`; every refund, cancel and re-request path requires it and no longer swallows a failed `cancel`. |
| 20 | medium | A vault request under a zero refund deadline (VRF) had no escape if the provider went silent. | Owner `setRequestRefundDeadline(requestId, seconds)`; only ever helps the beneficiary, still subject to #19. |
| 21 | medium | A plain `transferFrom` of an ACTIVE token into the vault (or an exit that named the vault as recipient) stranded the id and its principal. | `JerkzNFT` and `PackCollection` refuse any move into the vault the vault did not initiate; WorkRegistry exits refuse the vault as recipient. |
| 22 | medium | The pack blacklist check ignored the token owner and the pack NFT itself could be moved to a clean wallet. | Redeem checks the owner; `PackCollection` consults the underlying's blacklist on every transfer (`setUnderlying`). |
| 23 | medium | Mistake risk was frozen from the stress before the booked workload; spec §5.1 freezes the resulting stress. | Workload applied first, then the entry's stress and risk are frozen. |
| 24 | medium | The promotion gate required zero open entries, so a character that always had tomorrow booked was never evaluated and then jumped rungs. | Gate only holds when an open entry could still turn into a firing (`strikes + open ≥ 2`). |
| 25 | low-medium | A gacha spin from a contract that rejects native could never be refunded, freezing every withdrawal. | Refunds are credited to `refundOf` and pulled with `claimRefund`; anyone may trigger a refund after the deadline. |
| 26 | low | ERC-721 operators (marketplace approvals) could resign, apply, or clock a Jerk in with themselves as beneficiary. | Only the owner, or the custody beneficiary while in escrow, controls a career or a shift. |
| 27 | low | The custody beneficiary could never resign because any active shift blocked it, even after maturity. | Only unmatured shifts, open entries and unapplied results block. |
| 28 | low | `hireNow` cancelled a requested draw, admitted born executives, and could land a notice entry's strike on the new job. | Bypass only during warm-up; Intern–Director only; no open work or unapplied results. |
| 29 | low | Firing used the entry's work day as the notification day. | Notification day = the day the result is applied. |
| 30 | low | Mistakes halved the duration weight before the pay factor (rounding drift vs `floor(u×m/2)`). | Payroll computes `floor(u × m / 2)`. |
| 31 | low | A forfeited entry reset the career streak; the spec says forfeits pause it. | No reset. |
| 32 | low | A queued successor could redirect the beneficiary. | Successor inherits the chained shift's beneficiary. |
| 33 | low | The gate proof could itself be deposited as a prize. | Gate re-checked after custody. |
| 34 | low | `appoint` skipped health. | Burnout blocks appointment. |
| 35 | low | Gacha prize draw used `word % total`. | `RandomLib.sample` under its own domain. |
| 36 | operational | `processCohort(day, 100)` assessed at ~16.5M gas, at the RPC's estimate cap. | `MAX_PROCESS = 50`. |
| 37 | low | `setVault` could be repointed, breaking every holder's redemption. | Set once. |

Accepted, documented: a 20-character wrap settlement needs ~4.4M callback gas (size the Entropy
limit above it); a generation activated before the calendar's `supportedFrom` cannot work (any
replacement calendar must start no later than the earliest activation); entries on the calendar's
last day cannot settle (2036); continuous spinning delays gacha withdrawals by design; the gacha
reveal deadline is read live so the owner can open a refund window for a stuck spin.

## Shop review (same day)

Two reviewers on the testnet ItemShop: one on the contract and its role wiring, one on the shop
page's transaction flows. 13 contract proofs reproduced; all fixed. Wiring on testnet verified
(the shop holds exactly the SHOP role on WorkRegistry, EmploymentRegistry and JerkzNFT, and that role
grants nothing beyond the item hooks and `bumpMetadata`).

| # | Severity | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 38 | low-medium | `WorkRegistry._init` stored an idle-recovery projection without advancing `lastAccountedDay`, so buying a spouse for a Jerk that had never worked made later reads apply the same recovery again (stress under-reported, risk under-frozen). | `_init` records the last complete off day it already applied. Fixed; WorkRegistry, Payroll and the shop were redeployed the same evening (r4). |
| 39 | low | A "this week" pass bought Monday before 09:30 NY was for the weekend that had just ended. | Spec-consistent (weeks start Monday 09:30); the shop now exposes `passWeeks()` and the page labels each option with its actual Saturday date and disables a weekend that is over. |
| 40 | low | Remarrying the same persona burned 2,000 JERKZ for no change. | `AlreadyMarried`. |
| 41 | low | `divorce` ignored the catalog, so a repriced Divorce Papers still cost nothing. | `divorce` takes the catalog version and a max cost like every other item; v1 price stays 0. |
| info | | The custody beneficiary can divorce, dose or hire for the owner's Jerk while it is in escrow, and the owner cannot act until withdrawal. | Matches spec §9.1 "owner or fixed quest beneficiary"; the page shows only Jerks the wallet controls under that rule. |

Page fixes (jerkz-ui): JUICE and marriage were disabled for any custody shift instead of only an
unmatured one; the scanner listed strangers' custody Jerks as selectable; the hire button ignored
pending applications, open work and balance; quote deadlines were computed per render rather than
at signing; catalog state leaked across Jerk switches; public stats needed a wallet. All corrected.
